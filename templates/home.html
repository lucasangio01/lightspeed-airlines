Who we are

Founded by ex-cargo pilots who got tired of losing their ships to Imperial 'inspections', we built a company that puts passengers first, second only to keeping the engines running.

From smugglers' shortcuts to Senate-approved routes, we've flown them all, and kept most of our paperwork.

Our history

It started with a single freighter, a stubborn pilot, and a route nobody else was brave enough to fly.

Over the decades, we've expanded from one battered YT-1300 to a fleet spanning six star systems, and what began as a smuggling operation with excellent customer service slowly became a fully licensed transit company. Mostly.

In our early years, 'on time' meant 'before the bounty hunters caught up'. Today, it just means on time.
